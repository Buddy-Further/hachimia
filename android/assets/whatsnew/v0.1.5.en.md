Major cold-start frontend readiness speedup: eliminates the startup `agy` subprocess storm that saturated WebView's connection pool, staggers gateway maintenance sweeps, and removes blocking CookieManager disk flushes.

## What's New

- **Cold-Start SWR Disk Cache & Shared `agy models` Single-Flight**: Unified `agy models` execution across `models.mjs` and `login.mjs` with in-flight coalescing and disk caching (`~/.cache/hachimia-agy-models.json`, `~/.cache/hachimia-auth.json`); cold boot returns cached models and login status in milliseconds when local credentials exist and verifies silently after 1.5s, while deferring background `/usage` + `/credits` probes by 4s during the first 8s after gateway boot.
- **Non-Blocking Frontend Boot & Connection-Pool Relief**: Coalesced concurrent read-only `GET /api/login/status` and `GET /api/models` requests inside `api()` so WebView's 6-socket per-origin HTTP/1.1 pool is no longer starved while loading chat history; restored the `setOpen` guard on `renderProvCard()`; made `loadModels()` non-blocking inside `loadSettings()` so `boot()` completes immediately; deferred non-essential settings pre-fetches to 6s and dropped duplicate calls.
- **Staggered Gateway Startup Maintenance**: Split `server.mjs`'s synchronous 150ms post-listen sweep (which collided directly with WebView's initial request wave) into staggered 1500–2400ms idle slices.
- **Native Cookie Deduplication & Deferred Flush**: Deduplicated redundant `applyGatewayCookie` writes in `MainActivity` and moved `CookieManager.flush()` to a 250ms deferred call after `web.loadUrl()` begins once Chromium's in-memory CookieStore confirms the cookie.
